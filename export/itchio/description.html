⚔️ Beat And Match To Pass

Beat them all… or not. Beat the right enemies to open the way!

Gordon's path through the forest is blocked by electric barriers. Each barrier shows an enemy: an ant, a beetle or a spider. Only defeating that kind of enemy charges it, and once it is full, the way is open.

So you don't just mash buttons: pick your targets, mix quick and heavy strikes into deadly combos and save your lightning for the right moment! ⚡

👥 Better with a friend: play the whole adventure in two-player co-op on the same screen!

👥 Two-player co-op on one screen

Pick 2 PLAYERS in the menu and fight side by side through every level, bonus stages included.

  • 🎮 Any setup: two players on one keyboard, a keyboard + a gamepad, or two gamepads
  • ⌨️ Your keys, your way: AZERTY and QWERTY presets, every key and gamepad button can be remapped and tried out before the game starts
  • ⚡ A lightning storm each: every hero has their own life and mana bars, so time your storms together
  • 🔥 A tougher challenge: more barriers to open and enemies arriving faster, made for two
  • 🤝 Team play: shared score and kill streak, and enemies go after the closest hero, so split the crowd or bait them for your partner
  • 💫 Never out for long: a fallen partner gets back up at the next level, it's game over only when both heroes are down

✨ Features

  • 👥 Solo or two-player co-op on the same screen
  • 🗡️ Quick and heavy attacks: fast slashes or crushing overhead swings with a visible wind-up and stronger knockback
  • ⚔️ Nine combo chains: mix quick and heavy strikes to unleash an 8-cut flurry, alternating cross slashes or a ground slam that hits every nearby enemy
  • 💨 Dash: a quick dodge that makes you untouchable for an instant and cancels any swing
  • 🔥 Kill streaks: every 5 kills in a row raise your score multiplier, up to x4
  • ⚡ Lightning storm: spend your mana to call down three volleys of lightning on everything around you
  • 🐜 Three enemy types: ants swarm you, beetles hit hard, spiders shoot from a distance
  • 🐿️ Bonus stage: 15 seconds to hit the squirrels and grab extra life bottles
  • 🎯 Training mode: a harmless practice dummy that respawns endlessly, with the combo list on screen, solo or for two
  • 🏆 Best scores: chase your record
  • 🎵 Chiptune music, hit-stop, screen shake and particles that make every blow land

🎮 Controls

  • Arrow keys / D-pad: move
  • Enter / Space / A: quick attack
  • X / Y: heavy attack
  • Ctrl / B: lightning storm (needs mana)
  • Shift / X: dash
  • Esc / Start: pause
  • M: music on / off

👥 In two-player mode, each player gets their own keys (by default WASD / ZQSD + F R G H and arrows + L O K J) or a gamepad.

⌨️ Before each game you can test your keys in a small practice strip or remap them (keyboard and gamepad), also available from the settings. On-screen touch controls can be turned on for touch screens.

⚔️ Combos

Chain quick (R) and heavy (L) attacks: your inputs show up above your hero. Press the next attack as the current swing lands; quick strikes chain fast, heavy ones need a later press.

  • 🌪️ R R R R – Rafale: an 8-cut flurry
  • 💥 R R R L – Exécution: the strongest ground slam
  • ✖️ L R L – Croisé: six crossed slashes
  • 🪨 L L L – Fracas: a heavy ground slam
  • …and five more to discover, all listed in Training (C / LB)

Keep tapping during a flurry or cross finisher to see it through, and buffer your next press to flow straight into another chain.

💡 Tips

  • 👀 Look at the barrier icon before you fight: the wrong species won't charge it
  • 🛡️ Protect your streak: backing off for a second is often worth more than trading hits
  • 🪨 Ground slams for crowds: they hit every enemy around you on the same lane
  • 🌩️ Keep the storm for crowds: you're untouchable while casting
