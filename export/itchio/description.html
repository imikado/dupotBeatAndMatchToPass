⚔️ Beat And Match To Pass

Beat them all… or not. Beat the right enemies to open the way!

Gordon's path through the forest is blocked by electric barriers. Each barrier shows an enemy: an ant, a beetle or a spider. Only defeating that kind of enemy charges it, and once it is full, the way is open.

So you don't just mash buttons: pick your targets, keep your combo going and save your lightning for the right moment! ⚡

✨ Features

  • 🗡️ Four-hit sword combo: chain slashes, and land three combo hits on the same enemy for a score bonus
  • 💨 Dash: a quick dodge that makes you untouchable for an instant
  • 🔥 Kill streaks: every 5 kills in a row raise your score multiplier, up to x4
  • ⚡ Lightning storm: spend your mana to call down three volleys of lightning on everything around you
  • 🐜 Three enemy types: ants swarm you, beetles hit hard, spiders shoot from a distance
  • 🐿️ Bonus stage: 15 seconds to hit the squirrels and grab extra life bottles
  • 🏆 Best scores: chase your record
  • 🎵 Chiptune music, hit-stop, screen shake and particles that make every blow land

🎮 Controls

  • Arrow keys / D-pad: move
  • Enter / Space / A: sword attack (press again to chain the combo)
  • Ctrl / B: lightning storm (needs mana)
  • Shift / X: dash
  • Esc / Start: pause
  • M: music on / off

⌨️ Keys can be remapped from the settings, and on-screen touch controls can be turned on for touch screens.

💡 Tips

  • 👀 Look at the barrier icon before you fight: the wrong species won't charge it
  • 🛡️ Protect your streak: backing off for a second is often worth more than trading hits
  • 🌩️ Keep the storm for crowds: you're untouchable while casting
