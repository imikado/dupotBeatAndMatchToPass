# Décors de niveaux

Images créées avec l’outil intégré imagegen, à partir de `LevelTemplate/background-trees-up.png` comme référence de style. Recadrage des marges transparentes et réduction au plus proche voisin avec Godot, au format existant 620 × 63 pixels.

Alternance automatique : forêt originale, automne, marais, conifères, puis reprise du cycle. Le champ `biome` de ForestBackground permet de choisir une ambiance explicitement. Les palettes de ciel, brume et sol sont assorties dans ForestBackground.gd.

## Prompts

autumn.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent horizontal parallax woodland strip for an existing low resolution Godot side scrolling game. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its extremely simple tiny pixel art trees, flat limited color clusters, crisp blocky edges, brown branching trunks, no outlines, no detailed painting. An autumn woodland: irregular rounded ochre, muted copper and russet tree crowns, slender dark brown branching trunks, a few low stumps. Create ONE long horizontal strip, 1536x256 canvas, trees on a common baseline near bottom, crowns filling the height, around 20 varied small trees distributed across width. Transparent sky and transparent gaps between trunks; no sky, no background landscape, no ground slab, no text, no characters. Small low grass tufts at trunk bases. All trees fully inside canvas, transparent narrow margins at both ends so strip can repeat. Restrained dark midtone colors matching reference brightness, 3 shades per material maximum. This will be displayed at 620x64 game pixels; simple readable silhouettes essential. Actual transparent background.
